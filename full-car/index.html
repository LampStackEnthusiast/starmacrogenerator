02 / Half-car domain

Automatic sizing measures the imported CAD in STAR at playback.

Auto floor assumes the tires are the lowest points. Use flattened tire contact patches. A lower override creates a gap; an override cutting into the car is rejected. CAD is never moved.

Absolute lab coordinates in metres. X maximum is fixed at 0. Floor Z is Z minimum. These defaults reproduce the recorded enclosure; check them against your CAD.

Use in STAR-CCM+

  1. Import and name Body, the four wheels and both wings. Separate Headrest and Intake parts are optional. Confirm orientation and tyre contact geometry.
  2. Save an imported-only simulation with no regions.
  3. Download your named .java file, then play it as a macro.
  4. Inspect the domain and floor, mesh quality, air properties and force selections before solving.

Force reports are labelled Half: drag +Y and downforce −Z. Multiply each by 2 for a symmetric full-car estimate. Farfield and floor are excluded.
